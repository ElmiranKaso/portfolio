About Me

I am an Electrical Engineer with strong experience in electrical design, technical studies, and project supervision, combined with hands-on involvement in industrial and infrastructure projects.

My core expertise includes the study, calculation, and design of electrical systems such as substations (MV/LV), electrical distribution networks, WWTP (Waste Water Treatment Plants), and energy systems. I have worked across multiple industries including hospitals, marine applications, and heavy industry such as steel production.

I have experience in project execution, site supervision, and coordination of technical teams, ensuring that engineering solutions are implemented efficiently and according to specifications. My role has involved close collaboration with engineers, contractors, and multidisciplinary teams throughout all project phases.

Additionally, I have worked as a Sales Electrical Engineer, where I was responsible for technical project management, customer coordination, and supervising activities within the sales department. This experience strengthened my ability to combine technical knowledge with communication, organization, and leadership skills.

Currently, I am working in mission-critical data center environments, gaining experience in infrastructure operations, troubleshooting, and system reliability. My goal is to further develop in site engineering roles, combining design expertise with on-site execution and project supervision.
