About Me

I am an Electrical Engineer with a strong background in electrical design, technical studies, and project supervision, combined with practical experience in industrial and mission-critical environments.

My core expertise lies in the study, calculation, and design of electrical systems, including power distribution, protection systems, cable sizing, and technical documentation. I have worked on a wide range of projects such as substations (MV/LV), electrical distribution networks, WWTP (Waste Water Treatment Plants), and energy-related applications including PV systems.

I have extensive experience using engineering tools such as AutoCAD, Revit MEP, and EPLAN, as well as lighting design software like DIALux and Relux, enabling me to deliver accurate and efficient technical solutions.

Professionally, I have evolved into data center environments, where I have worked as a Data Center Technician, Customer Service Engineer, and Site Engineer. In these roles, I have been involved in mission-critical operations, infrastructure maintenance, troubleshooting, and coordination of technical activities.

My goal is to combine strong engineering design expertise with hands-on field experience to deliver reliable, efficient, and high-quality solutions in both industrial and data center environments.
